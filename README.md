# SFS-Texture-Pack-Generator

The Github repo for my SFS Texture Pack Generator. Not much else to say.

## Instructions

Open the exe. It should look like this.

<img width="641" height="432" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223727" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/39b1bf70-beda-4a0c-8121-5e64a8d411ab" />

Drag and drop your images(works best with square ones).

<img width="641" height="571" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223803" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/81d71294-15dc-477e-94cf-981d5144d955" />

Click "Generate Pack".

<img width="636" height="565" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223823" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/815e7223-ef5b-42d1-9b98-6194526719bb" />

The pack will be in your Documents folder

<img width="1266" height="665" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223840" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30f109de-5091-4723-b1cb-de5b9a24b7a3" />

Press CTRL + x and navigate to C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Spaceflight Simulator\Spaceflight Simulator Game\Mods\Custom Assets\Texture Packs and put it there

<img width="1266" height="665" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223840" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8407613-de25-477c-b8eb-9ef03cfcd096" />
<img width="1266" height="665" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223912" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2be83d36-ffa2-4728-87e0-98486d7bdd5c" />
<img width="1266" height="665" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223920" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bb07c82-ecd1-4e2c-ba30-35e97faa4c17" />
<img width="1266" height="665" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-26 223941" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1bd7c49-b301-497e-ab61-213fb390e9cf" />

And you have just made and instaled your very own texture pack, congrats!
